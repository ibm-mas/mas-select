How do I complete a work order?¤

New to Maximo Application Suite Select? Start with Technician: Complete your first work order for a guided introduction.

Open your work order. Do the work. Record what you did. Mark it complete.

The system tracks your time and parts automatically. Good records help everyone. They track costs and provide history for future work.

💡 Tips for success¤

  • Start work tracking as soon as you begin. Don't add time later.
  • Record notes and parts as you go, not at the end
  • Use clear, specific language so others can understand what happened
  • Take photos for anything unusual or hard to describe
  • Complete and close work orders promptly to keep queues accurate

Steps to complete work¤

Step 1: Find and open your work order¤

  • Go to the Technician app, from Work Orders > Technician on the desktop or in IBM Maximo Mobile. You can also use the Work Orders application.
  • Look for work assigned to you
  • Open the work order Need help finding your work? See How do I find my work?

Step 2: Start the work¤

  • Click Start Work to track time
  • Read the job plan or instructions
  • Check you have all parts and tools
  • Note any safety requirements

Step 3: Do the work¤

  • Follow the job plan steps
  • Complete any checklists
  • Take photos of issues or finished work
  • Note any problems you find

Step 4: Record what you did¤

Add work details:

  • Describe what you found and fixed
  • Be specific (helps the next person)
  • Note any follow-up work needed

Record materials:

  • Add parts from inventory
  • System tracks costs automatically
  • Request more parts if needed Learn how to issue parts to a job for detailed instructions on recording materials.

Confirm time:

  • System tracks time from when you started
  • Adjust for breaks if needed
  • Time is used for costs and planning

In practice

  • Many technicians wait until the end of the job to enter notes, which leads to missing or inaccurate details
  • Parts are often used but not recorded, causing inventory errors later
  • If time tracking isn't started at the beginning, reported labor is usually incorrect
  • Teams sometimes rely on memory instead of updating the work order in real time

Common mistakes

  • Writing vague notes such as "fixed issue" instead of describing the actual problem and repair
  • Forgetting to record parts or recording them later from memory
  • Leaving the work order in the wrong status (for example, still "In Progress")
  • Skipping photos or checklists when they are available
  • Not documenting additional problems discovered during the job

What good looks like

Poor note "Fixed filler"

Good note "Filler FL-02: Nozzle seal was worn and causing leaks. Replaced seal, cleaned nozzle assembly, tested operation. No leaks detected."

Step 5: Mark it complete¤

  • Click Complete Work when done
  • System stops tracking time
  • Work order goes to your supervisor
  • Supervisor closes it after review

What to include in your notes¤

Good notes help everyone:

  • What you found: "Seal on Filler FL-02 was worn and leaking"
  • What you did: "Replaced seal, cleaned nozzle assembly"
  • Result: "Filler running smoothly, no leaks"
  • Follow-up: "Monitor for leaks during next production run"

Using checklists¤

For inspections, go to the Inspections app, from Work Orders > Inspections on the desktop or in IBM Maximo Mobile. For more information, see How do inspections work?

If your work order has a checklist:

  • Complete each item as you go
  • Mark pass or fail for each point
  • Add notes for any failures
  • Take photos of issues
  • Finish all items before completing work

Working offline (mobile)¤

On the mobile app:

  • Start work without internet
  • Complete tasks and add notes offline
  • Take photos (saved on your device)
  • Changes sync when you're back online

For more detail, see What happens when I'm offline in IBM Maximo Mobile?

What happens after you complete work¤

After you mark complete:

  • Your supervisor gets notified
  • They review your work and notes
  • They close the work order if correct
  • Work order becomes part of equipment history
  • Costs are calculated and recorded

Why accurate records matter¤

  • If you do not record work details, asset history becomes unreliable
  • If parts are not recorded, inventory levels quickly become inaccurate
  • If time is incorrect, cost tracking and planning are affected
  • Poor data leads to repeated failures and inefficient maintenance

Having trouble completing work? Check What if I can't complete a task? for common issues and solutions.

Once this feels routine, you'll move through your work queue with confidence — and spend less time wondering what to do next.
