Technician: Complete your first work order¤

Get started quickly with a task designed for technicians like you. Learn the basics while accomplishing something useful.

Find and complete an assigned work order, for example, complete work on Filler FL-02.

Guided steps available: Prefer to learn in the application? Log in and open your Operational dashboard to follow guided steps for completing a work order.

💡 Tips for success¤

  • Start with a simple preventive maintenance task
  • Ask your supervisor if you're unsure about anything
  • Take photos to document your work
  • Record information as you go, not at the end

What to do¤

  1. Log into Maximo Application Suite Select.
  2. Go to Work Orders or My Assigned Work.

  3. Choose a simple work order to start (look for status "Assigned" or "In Progress"). New to work orders? Learn What is a work order? to understand the basics.

  4. Click to open the work order details.

  5. Change status to "In Progress".
  6. Review what needs to be done.
  7. Check if you need any parts or tools.
  8. Perform the maintenance task.
  9. Add notes about what you did.
  10. Record time spent (labor hours).

  11. Add any parts you used. Need to request parts? See How do I issue parts to a job?

  12. Update meter readings if applicable.

  13. Change status to "Completed".
  14. Verify all information is recorded.
  15. Submit for approval if required.

What success looks like¤

You've successfully completed your first work order when:

  • Work order status changed from "Assigned" to "Completed"
  • All required fields are completed such as notes, labor hours, or parts used
  • Your supervisor can see what you did and why
  • Equipment history now shows your work
  • Parts inventory updated automatically
  • Time and costs are recorded for planning

You'll know you're ready to move on when you can complete a simple work order from start to finish without help, and your notes clearly explain what you found and fixed.

For more detail on recording notes, parts, and time accurately, see How do I complete a work order?
