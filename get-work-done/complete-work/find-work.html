How do I find my work?¤

Use work queues to see all your assigned work in one place. Work queues group similar tasks so you can quickly find what needs to be done.

Work queues show only the work that matters to you. No searching through everything.

💡 Tips for success¤

  • Check your work queues at the start of each shift and periodically during the day
  • Always do urgent and overdue items first, then work by priority
  • Update work order status as soon as you start or complete work
  • Add notes immediately if you encounter issues or delays
  • Close completed work promptly so your queue stays accurate

When to use work queues¤

Work queues help you:

  • See all work assigned to you
  • Find urgent or overdue work fast
  • Focus on specific work types (inspections, repairs)
  • Track work for your team or location
  • Act on multiple items at once

How work queues work¤

Your administrator creates work queues that filter and show relevant work. Common types:

My work orders
All work orders assigned to you.

Overdue work
Work that should be done already.

High priority
Urgent work that needs immediate attention.

Team work
Work assigned to your crew or department.

Pending approval
Work waiting for your review.

Finding your work¤

Step 1: Open work queues¤

  1. Go to the Operational Dashboard
  2. Find the Work Queues section
  3. Each queue shows how many items it has

Step 2: Pick a queue¤

  1. Click on a work queue
  2. See all items with key details (status, priority, due date)
  3. Use filters to narrow down more if needed

Step 3: Open a work order¤

  1. Click on any item
  2. Read the work description
  3. Check what you need
  4. Start work when ready

In practice

  • Technicians often check work queues only at shift start, missing urgent work added during the day
  • Work orders sometimes sit in queues because no one checks regularly
  • Teams may rely on verbal assignments instead of using the system
  • Mobile users forget to sync, so they miss new assignments

Using the mobile app¤

On your phone or tablet:

  • Open the Maximo Mobile app
  • Go to your assigned work
  • Work orders sync automatically
  • Work offline if needed
  • Changes sync when you're back online

Common mistakes¤

  • Only checking work queues at the start of the shift
  • Ignoring priority and due dates when choosing work
  • Not refreshing the queue to see new assignments
  • Working on unassigned tasks without updating the system
  • Leaving completed work in your queue instead of closing it

What good looks like¤

Poor approach
Check queue once at 8 AM, work on whatever looks easiest, leave completed work open.

Good approach
Check queue at shift start and periodically throughout the day. Prioritize by urgency and due date. Update status as you progress. Close work promptly.

What if I don't see my work?¤

If work is missing:

  • Check that it's assigned to you
  • Check the work order status (might be on hold)
  • Refresh your work queue
  • Ask your supervisor if it's still missing

Why work queues matter¤

  • If you do not check queues regularly, urgent work gets delayed
  • If status is not updated, supervisors cannot track progress accurately
  • If completed work is not closed, queues become cluttered and unreliable
  • Poor queue management leads to missed deadlines and inefficient work distribution

Once you know where to look, finding your work takes seconds — and you'll never miss an assignment again.
