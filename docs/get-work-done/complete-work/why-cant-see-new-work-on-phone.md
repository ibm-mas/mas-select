# Why can't I see new work in IBM Maximo Mobile?

You were assigned a work order, but it does not appear in IBM Maximo Mobile. In most cases, the app is offline, your session has expired, or the app has not synced yet.

`[SME confirmation required: Confirm that these causes apply to MAS Select. The source is the Maximo Manage documentation.]`

## Quick reference

### Table 1. Causes and what to do

| Cause | What to do |
|-------|------------|
| The device is offline | Check your connection, then wait for the app to sync. |
| Your session has expired | Select the cloud icon to sign in again. |
| The app has not synced yet | Sync the app manually. |
| The work order is not assigned to you | Ask your supervisor to check the assignment. |
| Your administrator turned off data download | Ask your administrator to check the sync settings. |

## Likely causes

### The device is offline

IBM Maximo Mobile treats your device as offline when it is in airplane mode, the network adapter is turned off, the network bandwidth is low, the server cannot be reached, or your sign-in session has expired. While the device is offline, the app shows the data that is stored on the device and cannot receive new work.

**What to do**: Move to a place with a better connection, or turn off airplane mode. When the connection returns, the app syncs automatically. For more information, see [What happens when I'm offline in IBM Maximo Mobile?](../../start-fast/basics/what-happens-offline.md)

### Your session has expired

After your session expires, the app asks you to sign in again. Until you do, you work offline and new work does not arrive.

**What to do**: Select the cloud icon on the app screen and sign in again.

`[SME confirmation required: Confirm the cloud icon and the session lengths in Select.]`

### The app has not synced yet

While your device is online, the app syncs automatically. If you were assigned work a moment ago, it might not have arrived yet.

**What to do**: Sync the app manually, then check your assigned work again.

`[SME confirmation required: Confirm the name and location of the manual sync control in Select.]`

### The work order is not assigned to you

The work order might be assigned to another person, or it might not be in a status that appears in your assigned work.

**What to do**: Check the work order on the desktop, or ask your supervisor. For causes that apply on the desktop too, see [Why don't technicians see their assigned work?](../organize-people/why-cant-see-assigned-work.md)

### Your administrator turned off data download

Administrators set which data IBM Maximo Mobile downloads to devices. If the download of work data is turned off, new work does not appear after a sync.

**What to do**: Ask your administrator to check the sync settings. For administrators, see [How do I configure IBM Maximo Mobile for my team?](../setup-control/configure-maximo-mobile.md)

## Notifications about new work

Push notifications arrive only when you have an active session and have not signed out of the app. If you are not getting notifications, sign in again. For administrators, see [When users receive push notifications](../setup-control/automate-communication.md#when-users-receive-push-notifications).
