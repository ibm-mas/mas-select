# How do I get IBM Maximo Mobile?

IBM Maximo Mobile lets you view assigned work, complete work orders, assign and approve work, issue parts, receive inventory, and update records from your phone or tablet.

## 💡 Tips for success

- Download the app before your first shift, not during it.
- Sign in with the same credentials you use on the desktop.
- Sync your work list after signing in so your assignments are available on the device.

## What you need before you start

- A mobile device running iOS or Android.

  `[TO DO: Confirm whether Windows devices are supported in Select. Maximo Manage supports iOS, Android, and Windows.]`

- Your Maximo Application Suite Select login credentials.
- Your administrator must have configured IBM Maximo Mobile for your team. If the app is not yet available, contact your administrator.

## Download the app

1. Open the **App Store** (iOS) or **Google Play** (Android) on your device.
2. Search for **IBM Maximo Mobile**.
3. Download and install the app.

## Sign in

Your first sign-in needs a network connection. The app creates a local data store on your device, which it uses when you work offline.

1. Open **IBM Maximo Mobile** on your device.
2. If the app asks for a server URL, enter your Maximo Application Suite Select server URL. Your administrator can also set the URL for you, in which case the app does not ask.
3. Enter your username and password.
4. Tap **Sign in**.

`[SME confirmation required: Confirm the exact sign-in screens in Select, including single sign-on prompts.]`

## Sync your work

While your device is online, the app syncs your work automatically. You can also sync manually at any time. If your device is offline, the app uses the data stored on the device and syncs when it reconnects.

## Video transcript

Transcript of "Setting up the IBM Maximo Mobile app".

Maximo Mobile helps you take your work with you, so you can work in the field from your mobile device.

Start in Maximo Manage and open the Maximo Mobile Setup option. If you're already signed in, you can start setup from your profile menu. You get a QR code and a download link. Use either option to install the correct Maximo Mobile app for your device.

Open Maximo Mobile. The app prompts you to connect to your server. Connect by scanning the QR code or enter the server address your administrator provides. The app checks the address and connects.

Sign in with your user ID and password. After the server confirms your sign-in, Maximo Mobile downloads what you need for first use. Setup is complete.

You are ready to open Maximo Mobile and access your work in the field. Scan the QR code now to install the app.
