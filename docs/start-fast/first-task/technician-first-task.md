# Technician: Complete your first work order

Get started quickly with a task designed for technicians like you. Learn the basics while accomplishing something useful.

Find and complete an assigned work order, for example, complete work on Filler FL-02.

![](../../images/guided_steps_final.png){ width="16" .no-lightbox } **Guided steps available:**
Prefer to learn in the application? Log in and open your Operational dashboard to follow guided steps for completing a work order.

## 💡 Tips for success

- Start with a simple preventive maintenance task
- Ask your supervisor if you're unsure about anything
- Take photos to document your work
- Record information as you go, not at the end

## What to do

1. Log into Maximo Application Suite Select.
2. Go to **Work Orders > Work Orders**, or go to **Work Orders > Technician** and select **Assigned work**.
   <span class="see-also"><img src="../../images/info_light_mode.png" class="no-lightbox info-icon" alt=""> <span class="see-also-text">The Technician app is also available in IBM Maximo Mobile. Learn <a href="../basics/why-apps-overlap.html">Why can I manage work orders in more than one place?</a></span></span>

3. Choose a simple work order to start (look for status "Assigned" or "In Progress").
   <span class="see-also"><img src="../../images/info_light_mode.png" class="no-lightbox info-icon" alt=""> <span class="see-also-text">New to work orders? Learn <a href="../basics/what-is-work-order.html">What is a work order?</a> to understand the basics.</span></span>

4. Click to open the work order details.
5. Change status to "In Progress".
6. Review what needs to be done.
7. Check if you need any parts or tools.
8. Perform the maintenance task.
9. Add notes about what you did.
10. Record time spent (labor hours).
11. Add any parts you used.
    <span class="see-also"><img src="../../images/info_light_mode.png" class="no-lightbox info-icon" alt=""> <span class="see-also-text">Need to request parts? See <a href="../../get-work-done/manage-inventory/issue-parts.html">How do I issue parts to a job?</a></span></span>

12. Update meter readings if applicable.
13. Change status to "Completed".
14. Verify all information is recorded.
15. Submit for approval if required.

## What success looks like

You've successfully completed your first work order when:

- Work order status changed from "Assigned" to "Completed"
- All required fields are completed such as notes, labor hours, or parts used
- Your supervisor can see what you did and why
- Equipment history now shows your work
- Parts inventory updated automatically
- Time and costs are recorded for planning

**You'll know you're ready to move on when** you can complete a simple work order from start to finish without help, and your notes clearly explain what you found and fixed.

For more detail on recording notes, parts, and time accurately, see [How do I complete a work order?](../../get-work-done/complete-work/complete-job.md)

## What can I do in the Technician app?

The Technician app, from **Work Orders > Technician**, is built around the work that you do every day. It is the same app on the desktop and in IBM Maximo Mobile. In the Technician app, you can:

- View the work orders that are assigned to you, and filter them by priority, status, or due date.
- View work orders on a map to plan routes and reduce travel time.
- See work details such as job plans, instructions, and safety requirements.
- Record labor hours, tool use, and material use.
- Update the work order status as you work.
- Attach photos, notes, and documents to work orders.
- Report failures and update failure class information.
- Request materials, tools, or services.
- View details of the related asset and location.
- Add to the work log and review earlier entries.
- Create follow-up work orders.
- Record meter readings, such as energy use, run time, or fuel level.
