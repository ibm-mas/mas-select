# Working smarter

Find quick answers to common questions about Artificial Intelligence (AI), automation, scaling operations, and optimizing performance.

## AI and automation

### How can AI improve maintenance?

AI analyzes patterns in maintenance data to predict failures, recommend optimal maintenance timing, and identify efficiency opportunities. This shifts maintenance from reactive to predictive.

[I want to know more about AI in maintenance](../work-smarter/ai-automation/ai-improve-maintenance.md)

### What can I automate?

Automate work order creation from alerts, notifications for status changes, recurring maintenance scheduling, and data validation. Automation reduces manual effort and ensures consistency.

[I want to know more about automation](../work-smarter/ai-automation/what-can-automate.md)

## Scaling operations

### How do I manage more assets?

Use standardized processes, classification systems, and templates to handle growth efficiently. Automation and clear data standards become essential as asset counts increase.

[I want to know more about managing more assets](../work-smarter/scale-operation/manage-more-assets.md)

### How do I standardize across teams?

Establish common naming conventions, work processes, and data practices across all locations. Standardization enables consistent reporting and easier knowledge sharing.

[I want to know more about standardizing across teams](../work-smarter/scale-operation/standardize-across-teams.md)

## Insights and outcomes

### How do I turn data into decisions?

Use reports and dashboards to identify trends, compare performance across assets or teams, and spot opportunities for improvement. Good data visualization makes patterns visible.

[I want to know more about turning data into decisions](../work-smarter/insights-outcomes/data-into-decisions.md)

### How do I optimize performance?

Track key metrics, benchmark against targets, analyze what's working well, and continuously refine your maintenance strategy based on results. Optimization is an ongoing process.

[I want to know more about optimizing performance](../work-smarter/insights-outcomes/optimize-performance.md)

### What are KPIs in Maximo Application Suite Select?

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) provide measurable insight into operational and maintenance performance. They help track trends, evaluate outcomes, and support data-driven decision-making. Conceptually, KPIs act as a feedback mechanism across the system.

[I want to know more about metrics](../get-work-done/monitor-respond/what-metrics-matter.md)
