# Starting out

Find quick answers to common questions about getting started with Maximo Application Suite Select.

## Questions

### What can I do in my role?

Technicians complete work orders and record work, supervisors assign and track maintenance, inventory clerks manage parts and stock, and administrators configure data, access, and system settings. Each role focuses on key tasks to keep work moving.

[I want to know more about what I can do in my role](../start-fast/what-can-i-do/overview.md)

### What is an asset?

An asset is any piece of equipment, machinery, or infrastructure that your organization maintains. Assets are tracked in the system with details such as location, maintenance history, and current condition to help you manage their lifecycle.

[I want to know more about assets](../start-fast/basics/what-is-asset.md)

### What is a work order?

A work order is a request or instruction to perform maintenance work. It tracks everything about a maintenance job including what needs to be done, who does it, what parts are used, and when it's completed.

[I want to know more about work orders](../start-fast/basics/what-is-work-order.md)

### What is a location?

A location is a functional area or operating space where assets are installed or work is performed. Locations help you organize assets by where they're physically placed and track maintenance by area.

[I want to know more about locations](../start-fast/basics/what-is-location.md)

### Why do some terms differ from what I'm used to?

Maximo Application Suite Select uses a consistent set of terms to describe assets, locations, work, and inventory. These might differ from the terms you use today or in other systems.

[I want to know more about terminology](../start-fast/basics/why-do-terms-differ.md)

### What are classifications?

Classifications are used to structure and standardize how assets, items, and related records are described. They define which attributes apply to different types of records and ensure consistent data capture across the system. Conceptually, classifications establish a shared data model and vocabulary that supports reporting, maintenance strategies, and scalability.

[How do I classify assets?](../get-work-done/manage-assets/classify-assets.md)

### What are systems in the context of locations?

Systems provide a functional grouping of locations based on how assets work together. While locations describe physical placement, systems describe operational or functional relationships. This allows organizations to manage assets from both a physical and functional perspective.

### Why can I manage work orders in more than one place?

You can work with work orders in the Work Orders application, in the Technician app on the desktop, and in the Technician app in IBM Maximo Mobile. All three work with the same work orders.

[I want to know more about where to manage work orders](../start-fast/basics/why-apps-overlap.md)

### How do inspections work?

An inspection is a set of questions that you answer about an asset or a location. Inspections use two apps: Inspection Forms, to define the forms, and Inspections, to conduct inspections and record the results.

[I want to know more about inspections](../start-fast/basics/how-inspections-work.md)

### What happens when I'm offline in IBM Maximo Mobile?

IBM Maximo Mobile keeps working when your device loses its connection. The app stores your data on the device, so you can keep working and sync your changes later.

[I want to know more about working offline](../start-fast/basics/what-happens-offline.md)

### How do I get IBM Maximo Mobile?

IBM Maximo Mobile lets technicians, supervisors, and inventory clerks view assigned work, complete work orders, and update records from a phone or tablet.

[I want to know more about getting IBM Maximo Mobile](../start-fast/first-task/get-maximo-mobile.md)

### How do locations, assets, and work orders work together?

A location tells you where work happens. An asset tells you what equipment is maintained. A work order records the maintenance done on that equipment.

[I want to know more about how these concepts connect](../start-fast/basics/how-concepts-work-together.md)

### What keyboard shortcuts can I use?

Keyboard shortcuts let you save, move between records, change status, and navigate without switching between your keyboard and mouse.

[I want to know more about keyboard shortcuts](../start-fast/basics/keyboard-shortcuts.md)
