How do inspections work?¤

An inspection is a set of questions that you answer about an asset or a location. Inspections use two apps:

  • Inspection Forms, from Planning > Inspection Forms. Use it to define the forms that inspections use.
  • Inspections, from Work Orders > Inspections. Use it to conduct inspections and record the results.

Inspections is also in IBM Maximo Mobile, so you can inspect an asset or location where it is. The app has the same name on the desktop and in IBM Maximo Mobile.

[SME confirmation required: Confirm that the inspection behavior in this topic applies to MAS Select. The source is the Maximo Manage documentation. Confirm that Inspection Forms and Inspections are the app names and locations in Select.]

How inspections fit together¤

  1. A form defines the questions for an inspection.
  2. When the form is activated, an inspection appears in the Inspections app.
  3. An inspector answers the questions and completes the inspection.
  4. The results are saved with the asset or location that was inspected.

[SME confirmation required: Confirm how inspections are scheduled or generated from a form in Select, and where the results appear for the asset or location.]

What you can do in Inspections¤

  • Filter your inspections by pending, in progress, and completed.
  • Conduct several inspections at the same time, as a batch.
  • Create an unscheduled inspection when you find something that is not on your schedule, including for several assets or locations at once.
  • Show only the required questions, or only the questions that you have not answered.
  • Review your answers in a summary before you complete the inspection.
  • View the results of completed inspections.
  • Attach photos to an inspection.
  • Create follow-up work from an inspection.

The app saves your answers automatically as you go.

Who can use inspections?¤

The apps you see depend on your security group. In general:

  • Technicians complete inspections.
  • Supervisors review inspection results.

For the security groups, see How do I add users?
